Methodology

For each trend horizon (1m / 3m / 6m / 12m) the model computes standardised momentum, the log return over the lookback divided by its volatility-scaled standard deviation: z = ln(P/P₋ₗ) / (σ·√L). A response function tanh(k·z) maps trend strength to a position in [−1, +1], so exposure scales in smoothly and saturates toward max long / short on strong trends.

The composite is the weighted blend across horizons, reported as % of max exposure and as a $bn notional on an assumed gross trend-follower capacity. Trigger levels are the index prices where each horizon's momentum crosses zero; the aggregate flip level is solved numerically. Conditional projections evolve price under five scenarios and re-run the model forward, holding volatility fixed over the horizon.
