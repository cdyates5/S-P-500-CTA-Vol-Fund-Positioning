Methodology

Vol-control / target-vol funds size equity exposure inversely to realised volatility, holding portfolio risk near a target: exposure = clamp(target_vol ÷ realised_vol, 0, max_leverage). Realised vol is the annualised RMS of daily returns over each window (1m / 3m / 6m), blended with the weights at right. Because exposure is convex in 1/vol, vol crushes drive outsized re-leveraging (buying) and vol spikes drive outsized de-leveraging (selling).

Vol thresholds are the realised-vol levels at which exposure hits the leverage cap, 100%, 50% and 25% (= target ÷ exposure). Conditional projections evolve realised vol forward under five regimes (multiplicative on current 1-month vol); as the forward window fills, realised vol converges to the scenario level and exposure re-prices — the "flat" path still drifts as stale vol rolls out of the trailing windows. Notional and flows scale on an assumed complex AUM.
